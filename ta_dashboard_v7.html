All metric definitions below apply consistently across all pages of this dashboard. Where a target is defined, it is shown alongside the metric on the relevant page.
Hiring status
Total hires
Total number of candidates who have been onboarded in the selected period, including both external and internal hires.
Total openings
Total number of currently open Staff Requisition Forms (SRFs) that have been approved and are actively being worked on. Also referred to as "Total open SRF".
Median days open
The median number of days that current open SRFs have been open, measured from SRF approval date to today's snapshot date. Uses the median (not average) to reduce the impact of extreme outliers.
Overdue requisitions
SRFs where today's snapshot date has passed the target onboarding date and the position has not yet been filled.
Balance to source
The number of open SRFs that still require active sourcing. Formula: Current openings (A) − HC pending onboarding (B) − HC in offer process (C). Also the key measure of recruiter workload.
Hiring velocity
On-time hiring
Percentage of hires who were onboarded on or before their target onboarding date. Formula: hires on/before target date ÷ total hires × 100. KPI target: ≥80%.
Median time-to-offer acceptance
The median number of working days from SRF approval date to the date the candidate accepted the offer. Weekends are excluded; public holidays are included.
Median time-to-onboard
The median number of working days from SRF approval date to the candidate's actual onboarding date. Weekends are excluded; public holidays are included.
Throughput
Average number of hires completed per calendar month in the selected period. Formula: total hires ÷ number of months in the period.
Median LT to target onboard date
The median number of working days from SRF approval date to the SRF's target onboarding date. This is a forward-looking lead time for open SRFs, indicating urgency of sourcing. Uses the close SRF data source.
Cost & productivity
Total recruitment cost
Total spend on external recruitment activities in the selected period, sourced from Finance. Includes agency fees, job board subscriptions, and employee referral bonuses. Does not include internal TA team salaries.
Recruitment cost per hire
Total recruitment cost ÷ number of external hires in the selected period. Also referred to as cost per hire. KPI metric tracked against prior period.
Recruiter productivity
Number of external hires closed ÷ number of TA resources, expressed per month. Measures the output rate of the recruiting team. Source: Flash Home.
Recruiter workload
Balance to source ÷ number of TA resources. Measures the average number of open SRFs being actively sourced per recruiter at any point in time. Source: TA Hire Summary dashboard.
Hiring quality
30-day attrition
Percentage of hires who left the organisation within 30 days of their onboarding date. Formula: hires who left within 30 days ÷ total hires × 100. Source: Attrition dashboard.
60-day attrition
Percentage of hires who left the organisation within 60 days of their onboarding date.
90-day attrition
Percentage of hires who left the organisation within 90 days of their onboarding date. KPI target: below 10%.
No show rate
Percentage of confirmed joiners who did not report for work on their onboarding date. Formula: candidates who did not report ÷ total confirmed joiners × 100. Source: Flash Home.
Offer acceptance rate
Percentage of offers that were accepted by candidates. Formula: offers accepted ÷ offers extended × 100. Source: Flash Home.
SRF pipeline
SRF
Staff Requisition Form. The formal request to hire a new or replacement headcount, which must be approved before sourcing begins.
HC pending onboarding (B)
Headcount where an offer has been accepted and the candidate is confirmed to join, but has not yet started. These SRFs are effectively closed for sourcing purposes.
HC in offer process (C)
Headcount where an offer is currently being prepared or has been extended but not yet accepted or declined.
Target onboarding date
The date by which the hired candidate is expected to join, as specified in the SRF at the time of approval. Used to calculate on-time hiring and median lead time to target onboarding date.
New openings L7d
Number of new SRFs that were approved and entered the hiring funnel in the last 7 days. Signals demand addition to the pipeline.
New hires onboarded L7d
Number of SRFs that were closed by a successful onboarding in the last 7 days. Signals removal from the pipeline.
Data sources
TA dashboard
Primary Power BI dashboard. Source for on-time hiring and time-to-offer acceptance metrics.
TA Hire Summary
Weekly snapshot dashboard used for total openings, balance to source, and average hiring time. Designed for weekly management screenshots and reporting.
Flash Home
Internal TDCX operations dashboard. Source for recruiter productivity, no show rate, offer acceptance rate, and median lead time to target onboard date.
Attrition dashboard
Dedicated attrition tracking dashboard. Source for 30-, 60-, and 90-day attrition metrics.
Finance
Finance team data. Source for total recruitment cost figures. Project-level cost data not currently available.
BigQuery
Backend data warehouse. Historical snapshot data for current openings page is pending BigQuery integration, at which point date filters will be enabled for that page.
